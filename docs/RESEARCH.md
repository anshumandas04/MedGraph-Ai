# Research Document

## Problem Statement
Medical records are often fragmented, leading to poor continuity of care.

## Research Question
Can AI reconstruct a patient's healthcare journey from fragmented medical records and reliably identify important changes, conflicting information, and missing follow-up evidence?

## Hypothesis
An NLP pipeline combined with a temporal graph can accurately detect gaps and inconsistencies.

## Objectives
- Extract events.
- Reconstruct timeline.
- Identify signals.

## Methodology
- Synthetic data generation.
- LLM-based event extraction.
- Graph-based signal detection.

## Dataset
Synthetic benchmarks.

## Expected Outcomes
High precision in missing follow-up detection.

## Limitations
- OCR noise.
- Ambiguous language.

## Future Research
Integration with clinical ontologies.
